Cooku.com

Important: This Privacy Policy explains how Cooku.com collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cooku.com. Cooku.com is a grocery shopping and delivery application that allows users to browse grocery products, manage their shopping cart, save products to a wishlist, place orders, provide delivery information, and receive order-related notifications.

This Privacy Policy explains how Cooku.com collects, uses, stores, and protects information associated with your use of the Cooku.com application.

By using the Cooku.com application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Cooku.com, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Authentication information

If you use Google Sign-In, authentication may be provided through Google. Information made available through the authentication process may be associated with your Cooku.com account.

2.2 Location Information

Cooku.com may request access to your device's location to help determine your delivery location, identify an appropriate Cooku.com store, calculate delivery distance, and determine whether delivery is available in your area.

Location access is requested through the permissions provided by your device. You can manage or disable location permissions through your device settings.

If location permission is not provided, certain delivery location or store-related features may not work correctly.

2.3 Delivery Information

To process and deliver your orders, we may collect information that you provide for delivery, such as:

  • Delivery address
  • Area or locality
  • Town or city
  • Delivery instructions provided by you
  • Contact information required for delivery

2.4 Order Information

When you place an order, we may collect and store information related to the order, including:

  • Products ordered
  • Product quantities
  • Order amount
  • Delivery information
  • Order date and time
  • Order status
  • Payment method

2.5 Cart and Wishlist Information

Cooku.com may store information about products that you add to your shopping cart or wishlist so that these features can function correctly.

2.6 Device and Technical Information

The application and the services used by the application may automatically process certain technical information necessary for operation, security, diagnostics, and reliability.

This may include information such as device type, operating system, application version, network information, crash or diagnostic information, and other technical information made available by the services we use.

3. How We Use Your Information

Cooku.com may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Authenticating your account
  • Processing grocery orders
  • Preparing and delivering orders
  • Determining store availability
  • Calculating delivery distance
  • Determining delivery eligibility
  • Managing your cart and wishlist
  • Providing customer support
  • Sending order-related notifications
  • Improving application functionality
  • Maintaining application security
  • Detecting and preventing misuse or fraudulent activity
  • Maintaining business and transaction records
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

4. Google Sign-In

Cooku.com may provide Google Sign-In as an authentication option.

When you choose to sign in using Google, authentication is handled through Google's services. Information made available to Cooku.com through Google authentication may be used to create or access your Cooku.com account.

Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

5. Firebase Services

Cooku.com uses Firebase services to support certain application functions, including authentication, database functionality, notifications, and application infrastructure.

Depending on the features being used, information may be processed through Firebase services operated by Google.

Firebase may process technical information and information required to provide the requested application functionality.

6. Notifications

Cooku.com may use notification services to send important information about your account and orders.

Notifications may include information such as:

  • Order confirmation
  • Order status updates
  • Delivery-related updates
  • Important account information
  • Service-related announcements

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Payments

Cooku.com currently supports Cash on Delivery (COD) as a payment method.

When you choose Cash on Delivery, payment is collected according to the delivery process applicable to your order.

Cooku.com does not request or store your credit card, debit card, or online banking credentials through the application when Cash on Delivery is selected.

8. Sharing of Information

Cooku.com does not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share information when reasonably necessary to provide the services you request, operate the application, protect users, or comply with legal obligations.

This may include sharing relevant information with:

  • Delivery personnel or delivery partners when necessary to deliver your order
  • Service providers that help operate application infrastructure

  • Government authorities or law enforcement when required by applicable law

Cooku.com may use third-party services to provide application functionality. These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Third-party services may update their policies from time to time. Users should review the applicable policies of those services where appropriate.

You should also take reasonable steps to protect your account credentials and device.

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.
